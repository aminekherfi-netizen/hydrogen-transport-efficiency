### SLIDE 2: Introduction to Hydrogen

**Title:** What is Hydrogen?

**Content:**
- Hydrogen is an energy carrier, not a primary energy source
- Must be produced from other energy sources
- Environmental performance depends on production method
- Key properties:
  - Lightest element on Earth
  - High energy density by weight
  - Zero carbon emissions when used (only water produced)
  - Versatile applications

**Design:** Include a simple hydrogen atom diagram
**Notes:** Emphasize that hydrogen must be produced; it's not found in isolation

### SLIDE 5: How Fuel-Cell Vehicles Work

**Title:** Fuel-Cell Vehicle Operation

**Chemical Reaction:**
2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O + Electricity + Heat

**Process Flow:**
1. Hydrogen stored in high-pressure tank
2. Enters fuel cell
3. Electrochemical reaction produces electricity
4. Electricity powers electric motor
5. Only water vapor emitted

**Design:** Use a diagram showing the fuel cell process
**Animation:** Show the reaction step-by-step

**Key Advantage:** Zero local emissions

### SLIDE 10: Power-to-Hydrogen Process

**Title:** Power-to-Hydrogen (P2H)

**Process:**
1. Renewable electricity (solar/wind)
2. Surplus production occurs
3. Electrolyzer uses surplus electricity
4. Produces and stores hydrogen
5. Later use for electricity, heat, or transport

**Energy Flow:**
Renewable Electricity → Electrolyzer → Hydrogen Storage → End Use

**Equation:**
m_H₂ = (E_electricity × η_electrolyzer) / LHV_H₂

**Design:** Use flowchart diagram
**Color:** Green for renewable, blue for hydrogen

## Design Guidelines for PowerPoint

### Color Scheme
- Primary: Deep Blue (#003366)
- Secondary: Light Green (#00CC66)
- Accent: Orange (#FF9900)
- Background: White or light gray
- Text: Dark gray or black

### Fonts
- Title: Bold Sans-serif (Arial, Calibri, or Helvetica)
- Body: Regular Sans-serif
- Size: Titles 44-54pt, Body 18-28pt

### Layout Tips
- Keep text minimal (6-7 lines per slide maximum)
- Use visuals, diagrams, and charts
- Maintain consistent spacing and alignment
- Use bullet points, not paragraphs
- Include one main idea per slide

### Visual Elements
- Hydrogen molecule diagrams
- Vehicle images (buses, trucks, trains)
- Energy flow diagrams
- Bar charts and comparison tables
- Icons for applications
- Process flow diagrams
- Map showing hydrogen infrastructure

### Speaker Notes (for each slide)
Include detailed talking points that expand on the slide content but are not shown to the audience.
